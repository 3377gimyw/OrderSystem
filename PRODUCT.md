# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Bar customers (Korean, mobile) who scan a QR code at their table. Goal is fast, frictionless ordering — open the link, pick items, submit. No login, no payment friction. Ambient context: low-lit bar, one hand on phone.

## Product Purpose

Mobile ordering system for 403 Forbidden bar. Replaces verbal ordering so staff can focus on service. Orders land in Google Sheets in real time. Success = customer submits order in under 60 seconds from QR scan.

## Brand Personality

Cold, deliberate, forbidden. Named after an HTTP error code — the aesthetic is tech-native without being gimmicky. Not a nightclub, not a food-delivery app. A bar with a point of view.

## Anti-references

- Bright/cheerful Korean food-app UIs (배달의민족-style yellow, rounded bubbles, cartoonish icons)
- Nightclub glam (neon glows, particle effects, excessive animation)
- Hacker terminal pastiche (green-on-black, blinking cursors, movie-prop tech)

## Design Principles

1. **Speed over delight** — every screen gets out of the way. The order is the destination, not the app.
2. **Cold confidence** — the UI doesn't ask for approval. Deliberate typography, flat surfaces, no decorative chrome.
3. **Name earns its weight** — "403 Forbidden" is the brand, not a joke. Treat it with the same weight as a real brand name.
4. **Red means action** — the accent color is reserved for interactive affordances, not decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA. Body text 4.5:1 minimum, large/UI text 3:1 minimum. Red accent used on backgrounds only, never as foreground text on white/light surfaces.
